The levels

Pullback line

Buys: the last H4 low that broke a high. Sells: the last H4 high that broke a low. It only counts after a real pullback, not one candle.

Pullback-break range

The leg that broke the pullback line. Price often comes back into it. It holds: H4 is still against the Daily. It breaks: the Daily continues.

Last H4 high

The high that made the external low. Until it is taken, H4 is still bearish. Taken = back in alignment with the Daily.

Daily range

Set by the first pullback after the Daily breakout. Premium above the 0.5, discount below. Sells get careful in the discount.
