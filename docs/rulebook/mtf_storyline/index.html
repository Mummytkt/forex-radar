The frame timeframe gives the external high and low and the breakout. The story timeframe tells the pullback. The entry timeframe confirms the trade. The rules below use the Daily story; the other plans work the same way one step up or down.

How a break counts

Daily

A Daily body close beyond the level's wick.

H4

An H4 body close beyond the level's body.

Only External breaks

A level counts only when an External break made it, as in the Bias Pointer. A tiny candle with no pullback is not a high or a low.

Inside the Daily range

The H4 range high and low always sit inside the Daily range.

The rules

1

The Daily breakout sets the frame

A Daily candle body closes beyond the Daily external high (the wick level). The Daily external high and low are the Bias Pointer's, always. Every new Daily breakout starts the story again in its direction.

On the chart: D.BO · D.Bull XH / XL · alert: Daily breakout

2

Continuation: trade the breakout direction

In a bullish move lows break highs. The pullback line is the low that broke the last high. Until that high breaks, the low stands and everything inside it is ignored. A high counts once price really pulled back from it (a candle's wick took out the previous candle's low; after a bearish candle, one candle can do this and close above the high, and its wick is then the new line). When an H4 body closes above it, the line moves to the last low inside the breaking move that broke a high; else the new dip low if it is above the current line; else the broken high itself. The line is drawn on the wick.

On the chart: H4.C · PL · alert: H4 continuation after the Daily breakout

3

Pullback break: the Daily pulls back

An H4 body closes below the pullback line. The Daily pullback is confirmed, the Daily range prints its premium and discount, and H4 is now against the Daily. The new pullback line is the closest high inside the range just broken. The H4 range resets to that range: one side is the top of the leg, the other is the newest leg extreme beyond the old line.

On the chart: PB · D.PC · D.EQ · alert: H4 pullback confirmed

4

Trade the pullback's continuation, mind the discount

While H4 is against the Daily, each close below a real low is an H4 bearish cont, and the H4 pullback line moves to the highest point of the leg that broke that low. Selling deep in the Daily discount (below 0.5) is where the Daily may take over again.

On the chart: H4.C pb · H4.PL · alert: H4 continuation after the pullback

5

The level that must hold

After a pullback, the last high created before it is expected to hold. It holds: H4 is still in play against the Daily. It breaks on an H4 body close: the H4 pullback is over.

On the chart: (the decision point, no label of its own)

6

H4 pullback: back in alignment, the H4 range

An H4 body close above the H4 pullback line is the H4 pullback. H4 is back in alignment with the Daily. The new pullback line is the closest low inside the range just broken. The H4 range resets again: one side is the far end of the move against the Daily, the other is the newest leg extreme beyond the old line, always inside the Daily range.

On the chart: H4.P · BIA · H4.RH / H4.RL · alert: H4 pullback: back in alignment

7

Inside the H4 range the pullback line keeps trailing

Inside the range the same rule goes on: each real high broken moves the pullback line up to the low that broke it. Everything else inside is ignored.

On the chart: PL · H4.C al · PB.in · H4.C in · alerts: H4 continuation in alignment, Inside the H4 range

8

The range breaks: the Daily continues (or H4 keeps going against)

A close above the H4 range high is the last H4 high inside the Daily range taken: now you can be convinced you are with the Daily. A close below the H4 range low instead means H4 is against the Daily again. Both are sent as H4 continuations.

On the chart: H4.RHB · D.C / H4.RLB / one candle through the line and the range edge: H4.P+C · alerts: H4 continuation

Reading the pullback: hold or break

Every decision in the story is the same question at a different level: does the last high or low before the pullback hold?

It holds

H4 is still in play against the Daily. Keep trading the H4 continuation, and be careful in the Daily discount (buys) or premium (sells).

It breaks

The H4 pullback is over. H4 may be back in alignment with the Daily: this is the H4 pullback tag.

At the last H4 high inside the Daily range

It holds: you may still be against the Daily. It breaks: now you can be convinced you are with the Daily (Daily continues).

Where it meets the Bias Pointer

Both scripts use the same Daily external high and low and the same H4 External break. The Bias Pointer asks where the Daily pullback should stop: a reaction at a Daily OC, A or V-shape, FVG or Breaker inside the Daily range (or a post-breakout level), then an H4 External breakout with the Daily confirms it. The MTF Storyline tells the structure around it: when the pullback started, how far H4 went against the Daily, and when it came back in alignment.
